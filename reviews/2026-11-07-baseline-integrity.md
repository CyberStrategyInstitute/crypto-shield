## Coverage and evidence

The [file manifest](coverage-manifest.json) records each baseline path and review disposition. JSON and control inventories were examined structurally; registry descriptions, control references, scoring rows, and documentation were reviewed for substantive consistency. The PNGs were visually inspected. This is not primary verification of every embedded external claim. Questionable figures and causal or legal conclusions were withdrawn from active guidance rather than promoted from the supplied analysis.

[The evidence ledger](evidence-ledger.json) records narrow original-source checks for npm commands, ERC-2612 semantics, recovery-fraud guidance, the CTA example, and repository inventory. No original recent Binance, Polymarket, Ledger, Initia, X100, or other incident disclosure is adopted as an independently validated case in this update. No product, wallet, bridge, smart contract, or endpoint implementation was deployed or adversarially qualified.

Counts, schema acceptance, rejection fixtures, reference generation, and link checks are automated. Acceptance tests for financial authority, human approval, hardware integrity, bridge backing, governance capture, and physical safety are specifications requiring future implementation-specific evidence. Do not confuse passing repository checks with those outcomes.
