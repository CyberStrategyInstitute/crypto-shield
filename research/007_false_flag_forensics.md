## Finding

Attribution, privileged access, responsibility, and recoverability need separate evidence. One label cannot answer all four.

## Method and evidence

Cases were selected for explanatory value, source accessibility, and category coverage, not population representativeness or loss ranking. The reports link original publisher material and record its dates and limitations. Source review is not independent technical replication.

- [Bybit major theft: official attribution and response boundary](../intelligence/reports/bybit-official.md)
- [Coinbase support-data theft and extortion disclosure](../intelligence/reports/coinbase-support.md)
- [Colonial Pipeline ransom-proceeds seizure](../intelligence/reports/colonial-recovery.md)

## Analysis

Use source classification and explicit competing hypotheses. The inspected accounts show why official attribution, an affected-party insider account, and a lawful seizure account have different evidentiary roles. They do not calibrate a false-flag probability. A source's incentive is a reason to seek corroboration, not proof of deception.

## Control consequences

CDG-06 records evidence and limitations; CDG-07 governs any future assessment model. Retain unknown values. Separate an employee deceived by an external actor from a deliberately malicious employee, and separate compromised infrastructure from organizational negligence.

## Worked application

Review a case with the source checklist, then ask a second analyst to reproduce only the supported claims. The extortion exercise requires distinguishing seizure from reversal and restitution. No weighted FFPS or automatic three-indicator rule is adopted.

Use the [tabletop pack](../tools/tabletop-exercises.md), [playbook guide](../playbooks/README.md), and [operating guide](../OPERATING-GUIDE.md). Case facts belong in reports; reusable classes belong in taxonomy; operational decisions belong in incident logs.

## Counterexamples and remaining work

Build a labeled evaluation set with independent adjudication, contradictory evidence, outcomes and revision history before considering quantitative attribution. Include successful recovery despite finality and failures despite controls. The full historical FFAP project remains incomplete.

No CTRS rating, calibrated attribution probability, population statistic, or prevention percentage is established.
