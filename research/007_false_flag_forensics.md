# Research Note 007: Attribution evidence review

Status: Seed methodology under revalidation; full report incomplete. Integrity review: November 7, 2026. Threats: CS-INS-01, CS-INS-03, CS-DFI-01.

This update does not complete the historical investigation. Earlier incident amounts, attribution conclusions, calibrated-score claims, and prevention estimates are not adopted as verified findings. The [original note](https://github.com/CyberStrategyInstitute/crypto-shield/blob/051a317fb72db38f11d8928fddd5c506d24d468a/research/007_false_flag_forensics.md) remains in Git history for audit.

## Evidence needed

The former weighted FFPS, probability tables, and case probabilities are withdrawn as uncalibrated. Record hypotheses, supporting and contrary evidence, source access, alternative explanations, and qualitative confidence. Seek cases that disconfirm the preferred explanation. Establish inter-rater reliability before considering any numerical attribution model.

## Interim guidance

A compromised employee can be a victim of an external actor. Actor attribution and organizational responsibility are separate questions; neither automatically establishes legal liability.
