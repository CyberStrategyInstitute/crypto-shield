## Finding

A market-risk explanation needs a mechanism and evidence. Synchronized opinions, a price decline, or a beneficiary do not establish a coordinated extraction operation.

## Method and evidence

Cases were selected for explanatory value, source accessibility, and category coverage, not population representativeness or loss ranking. The reports link original publisher material and record its dates and limitations. Source review is not independent technical replication.

- [Mango Markets price and collateral manipulation allegations](../intelligence/reports/mango-market.md)
- [BitConnect investment-fraud indictment](../intelligence/reports/bitconnect.md)

## Analysis

Separate valuation/market-liquidity manipulation from token allocation, unlock concentration, promotion, ordinary trading, and fraud allegations. The selected official allegations illustrate distinct mechanisms and must not be merged into one proven conspiracy. The prior Rotator Effect hypothesis remains a research hypothesis.

## Control consequences

CS-MKT-04 now covers price/collateral manipulation explicitly. AAS-09 and OCM-11 require tests of valuation independence, liquidity, concentration, and combined exposure. GCS-12 covers lasting authority obtained through temporary governance influence; it is not a substitute for market evidence.

## Worked application

Use the market exercise: inject illiquid collateral appreciation and a withdrawal request. Record data provenance, price age, liquidity assumptions, exposure reservation, authority to pause, and a decision that does not presume criminal intent.

Use the [tabletop pack](../tools/tabletop-exercises.md), [playbook guide](../playbooks/README.md), and [operating guide](../OPERATING-GUIDE.md). Case facts belong in reports; reusable classes belong in taxonomy; operational decisions belong in incident logs.

## Counterexamples and remaining work

Reconstruct transaction-level positions, voting records, communications, price sources, and later legal outcomes. Test alternative explanations and seek negative cases where unusual price movement was not manipulation.
