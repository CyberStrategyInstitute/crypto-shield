# Ransomware, blackmail, or extortion

Use during: Threats to publish data, harm people, disable systems, or demand payment. This is an adaptable response procedure; only authorized responders should operate production controls. Physical safety comes first.

## Observe and classify

Open a decision record using the [incident log](../tools/templates/incident-log.json). Assign incident lead, technical responder, evidence owner, and communication/legal owner as appropriate. One person may cover roles, but preserve independent approval for consequential changes.

## Contain within authority

1. Prioritize immediate physical safety and contact emergency responders when safe. Preserve the demand and use independent communication paths.
2. Isolate affected systems when appropriate, identify credential and financial exposure, and engage incident responders and qualified counsel. Do not negotiate or make payments through a generic automatic instruction.
3. Preserve hashes, chain, dates, recipient details, original messages, system logs, and reporting records. Contact relevant authorities and independently verified financial intermediaries.

## Stop conditions and escalation

Payment does not guarantee recovery or cessation. Lawful tracing or seizure is different from reversal, compensation, or restitution; no outcome is promised. If exposure, authorization, or a safe action cannot be established, isolate the uncertain path and escalate to an independently verified qualified responder. Never request or disclose seeds or private keys in a report.

## Verify and hand off

Verify containment, restored systems and authority, reporting references, and any claimed financial return. Keep seizure, return, reimbursement, and unrecovered loss separate.

Record owner, action, UTC time, evidence, expected result, observed result, residual risk, and approval to resume. Do not mark success merely because an action was attempted. Use the [tabletop pack](../tools/tabletop-exercises.md) to rehearse without live funds or real emergency notifications.
