Use during: A provider, issuer, court, regulator, or contract authority changes access, redemption, freezing, or reporting. This is an adaptable response procedure; only authorized responders should operate production controls. Physical safety comes first.

## Observe and classify

Open a decision record using the [incident log](../tools/templates/incident-log.json). Assign incident lead, technical responder, evidence owner, and communication/legal owner as appropriate. One person may cover roles, but preserve independent approval for consequential changes.

## Contain within authority

1. Record the exact notice, source, date, jurisdiction, entity, asset, effective time, and action claimed. Verify authenticity independently.
2. Identify key control separately from legal title, custody liabilities, redemption, issuer/upgrade powers, and alternative access paths. Obtain qualified legal review for the specific activity.
3. Pause new exposure if obligations or access cannot be established. Preserve contracts, statements, reserve/liability evidence, notices, and decision records; use authorized support and complaint routes.

## Stop conditions and escalation

A restriction is not automatically unlawful, and delisting does not establish universal permission. Do not move assets to evade lawful restrictions or infer solvency from reserves alone. If exposure, authorization, or a safe action cannot be established, isolate the uncertain path and escalate to an independently verified qualified responder. Never request or disclose seeds or private keys in a report.

## Verify and hand off

Document applicable authority, permitted actions, custody/redemption evidence, remaining dependencies, and an accountable decision owner before resuming.

Record owner, action, UTC time, evidence, expected result, observed result, residual risk, and approval to resume. Do not mark success merely because an action was attempted. Use the [tabletop pack](../tools/tabletop-exercises.md) to rehearse without live funds or real emergency notifications.
