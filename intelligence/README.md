## How to use a case

Read the source and limitations, inspect the event, then route a comparable situation to the playbook. Research notes synthesize the control lesson; the taxonomy classifies it; tools capture authority, evidence, decisions, and verification. Do not run historical indicators or a teaching fixture as a live rescue instruction.

All report drafts need independent review before being treated as final CSI intelligence. Later legal outcomes and technical corrections are not implied by a historical source date. Recent cases from the supplied analysis remain a separate research backlog.
